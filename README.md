# Amazon_products_sales_analysis
PowerBI Dashboard 

Objective:
To transform Amazon sales data into an interactive dashboard that helps users understand sales trends, category performance, product performance, and customer review activity for better business analysis and decision-making.

Tools: Power BI, DAX, Data Visualization, Data Modeling

 Key Features

1. YTD Sales, QTD Sales, YTD Products Sold, and YTD Reviews KPI cards
2. Monthly Sales Trend to track sales performance throughout the year
3. Weekly Sales Analysis to identify sales patterns and peaks
4. Sales by Product Category with YTD and QTD sales comparison
5. Top 5 Products by YTD Sales
6. Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews
7. Interactive Product Category and Quarter filters
8. Dark-themed dashboard with clear KPI and chart visualizations
